# Home Page Design Override

This page-specific override inherits the shared rules in `../MASTER.md`.

## Theme

- Use a cinematic dark-only palette: background `#080A11`, raised surfaces `#111522` and `#151A29`, primary text `#F6F7FB`, and muted text `#A8B0C3`.
- Use violet `#8A7CFF` for primary actions and highlights, with mint `#70E6D1` as a restrained secondary accent.
- Keep borders subtle and surfaces layered; reserve stronger glow and contrast for primary actions and featured pricing.

## Typography and layout

- Use Space Grotesk for display headings, Inter for body and controls, and DM Mono for eyebrow labels and technical details.
- Preserve the existing responsive marketing-page sections, generous spacing, readable content measure, and mobile single-column layouts.

## Interaction

- Use short color, border, and shadow transitions without shifting surrounding layout.
- Keep keyboard focus visible and preserve the existing reduced-motion behavior.
- Maintain readable text contrast across cards, pricing, form fields, navigation, and the floating contact action.
